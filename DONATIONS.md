# Voluntary donations

If you find MM Zidoo Sync useful, please consider a voluntary donation to support its development and maintenance.

A donation is optional. It is not required to use, modify, redistribute or sell the software under GPLv3, and it does not purchase priority support or a warranty. To donate, use Zelle through your participating bank and send to **magrimsley@hotmail.com**. Confirm that Zelle displays the intended recipient before sending. This email is a payment identifier, not a checkout link. Donations are not represented as tax-deductible charitable contributions.
